# Label Guide

Hareeg Table uses emoji-prefixed labels so issue and PR lists are easy to scan.

## Type

- `📌 prd`: Parent PRD with scope, stories, decisions, and child tracker.
- `🧩 feature`: User-visible vertical slice or playable product capability.
- `🐛 bug`: Confirmed defect, regression, crash, or incorrect behavior.
- `📚 docs`: Repo docs, in-app help, rules docs, or templates.
- `🎨 design`: Visual design, interaction design, tone, layout, or animation.
- `🧪 test`: Test coverage, QA scenarios, goldens, or verification-only work.
- `🧹 chore`: Tooling, dependencies, repo hygiene, or structure.
- `❓ question`: Product, rule, technical, or design decision needed.

## Area

- `🃏 cards`: Cards, deck copies, melds, hands, or card rendering.
- `🔥 fifty`: Fifty/Khamsin claim flow, timer, scoring, or visuals.
- `🃟 jokers`: Joker identity, placement, replacement, restrictions, or visuals.
- `📏 rules`: Classic Hareeg rule engine, validation, scoring, or presets.
- `🧠 ai`: CPU strategy, difficulty, personality, or reaction timing.
- `🖥️ ui`: Flutter screens, navigation, controls, or table layout.
- `💾 persistence`: Local storage for settings, preferences, match state, or history.
- `🌍 localization`: English/Arabic strings, terminology, RTL readiness, or translations.
- `⚙️ settings`: Setup screens, rule customization, or user preferences.
- `🚦 ci`: CI, analysis/test automation, builds, or toolchain health.

## Status

- `🧭 needs triage`: Needs prioritization, labels, scope, or owner review.
- `✅ ready`: Scoped, unblocked, and ready to start.
- `⛔ blocked`: Waiting on a dependency, decision, asset, or prerequisite.
- `👀 needs review`: Ready for human/code review before merge or closure.
- `🙋 hitl`: Requires human judgment for rules, naming, design, tone, or product behavior.
- `🚧 in progress`: Actively being worked on.

## Priority

- `🚨 p0`: Critical blocker for viability, correctness, or release.
- `⚡ p1`: High-priority work for the next milestone or core path.
- `🔹 p2`: Normal-priority work.
- `🧊 p3`: Low-priority cleanup or later improvement.

## Scope

- `🏗️ foundation`: Architecture, CI, project setup, shell, and scaffolding.
- `🎮 playable loop`: Complete playable Classic Hareeg round/match loop.
- `✨ polish`: Usability, visuals, accessibility, performance, and player feel.
- `🔮 future`: Later-release work such as Hareeg 14, Fifties mode, or online play.
