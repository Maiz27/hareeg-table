## What the app is

Hareeg Table is a free card game for playing Classic Hareeg offline against three
computer players. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no in-app
purchases. Your games, settings, and match history are stored only on your
device.

The only thing the app ever sends over the internet is an optional crash and
bug report, described below.

## Data we do not collect

Hareeg Table does not collect, and does not ask for:

- your name, email address, phone number, or any account details;
- your location;
- your contacts, photos, files, or messages;
- the advertising ID or any other identifier used for advertising or tracking;
- payment information (there is nothing to buy);
- your app settings, language preference, or match history (except the single
  match report described below, when a report is sent).

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or
share data for advertising.

## Crash and bug reports (Sentry)

To find and fix bugs, the app can send a diagnostic report to us through
Sentry, a crash-reporting service. A report is sent when:

- the app hits an error or crash;
- a game gets stuck and the app recovers it automatically; or
- you choose **Report table issue** or **Export match report** and tap
  **Send report**.

A report contains:

- **Error details:** the error message and the technical trace of where in the
  app's code it happened, plus a short trail of recent app events leading up
  to it.
- **App and device details:** the app version, the device model and
  manufacturer, the operating system and its version, and general technical
  state such as screen size, memory, and whether the app was in the
  foreground.
- **A match report file:** the state of the game on the table (the cards,
  scores, game setup, and the moves played in that match) so we can replay the
  problem. It contains no personal information: no names, no settings, no
  language, and nothing you typed.

The app is configured so that reports do **not** include your name, a user
identifier, the device's name or unique device identifier, your language or
time zone, screenshots, or your IP address. Sentry's automatic collection of
personal information is turned off, and IP addresses are not stored.

Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. If your device is
offline, a report may be held on the device and sent the next time you are
connected.

## Your choice: consent and opt-out

The first time you open the app, it tells you about crash and bug reports and
lets you keep them on or turn them off. Nothing is sent before you have seen
this notice.

You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy > Send crash &
bug reports**. When reports are turned off, the app sends nothing at all: the
reporting service is not started, and any running reporting is shut down. You
can still save a match report and share or copy it yourself, which sends
nothing unless you choose where to send it.

## How we use reports

We use reports only to find, reproduce, and fix bugs in Hareeg Table. We do
not use them to identify you, profile you, or contact you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the
right to access, correct, or delete data about you, or to object to its use.
Because reports contain no name or user identifier, we usually cannot link a
report to you. If you want a report deleted, email [support email] with the
approximate date and time you sent it and we will do our best to find and
delete it. You can also turn reports off at any time, as described above.

The legal basis for processing reports is our legitimate interest in keeping
the app working, and you can opt out at any time.
